Before You Visit

Visitor Guidelines & Safety

Mandatory

Register at the Reception

All visitors must present a valid ID and sign in at the faculty reception before entering any laboratory.

Wear Protective Equipment

Lab coats, safety goggles and closed shoes are provided and required inside all laboratory areas.

Stay With Your Guide

Do not touch equipment or chemicals. Always remain with your assigned guide throughout the tour.

Photography Policy

Photos are permitted in common areas only. Ask your guide before photographing any equipment or documents.

Need Special Arrangements?

For group visits over 20 people, accessibility needs or media coverage, contact the faculty office at least 5 working days in advance.
